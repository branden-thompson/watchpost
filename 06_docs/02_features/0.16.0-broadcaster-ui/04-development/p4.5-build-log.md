## Three defects from this batch worth carrying forward

**1. A hidden column still emitted its cell.**  go-studs answered *"Data length does not match column
count"* **in place of the row** — so hiding WX STN blanked every location.  A renderer that reports a
structural error INTO the cell it was asked to draw makes a layout bug look like a data bug.

**2. Two carriers of one fact, three times.**  `fillPoolWeather` copied 6 of 16 fields and the other
ten drew `n/a`; `bcPriorityWidth` outlived the function it was derived from; the air box's width had a
second owner and the `b` chip fell off the row.  All three are the same shape, and the same shape
closed the batch (D-122's frozen tie, the fire threshold's restated default).

**3. The light theme was not painting text white — it was not painting it at all.**  Fixed by arming
`TextBase` in `clamp` via `FgSGR`, NOT `TintDefault`, which hard-codes `38;5;` and breaks Light's
truecolor.
