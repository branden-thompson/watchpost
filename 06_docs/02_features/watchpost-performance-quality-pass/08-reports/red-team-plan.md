## Verdict

**SHIP-WITH-CONDITIONS** — unanimous across the four axes, the PLAN lens and all five personas; both
probes concur. The architecture holds (every lens verified the cache-tier, width-authority, tick-gating
and patch-ledger *intent* against code); what failed review was the plan's **specification**: ten
Critical findings (nine defects — PA-1 and PF-3 are the same one), every one a plan-text or apparatus
fix rather than a design change. The conditions are the Critical rows below, all of which are now
applied in `03-architecture-design/quality-pass-plan.md` v2 (ledger column "Fixed" cites the section).
Nothing is dropped: **134 raw findings** (10 formal lenses: 10 Critical / 53 Important / 43 Minor or
verified; 2 probes: 7 HIGH / 14 MEDIUM / 7 LOW-INFO) consolidate into 24 themed findings; the ledger
maps every raw ID.

Criticals (9 defects): JD-1 patch process destroyable by resync · PA-1/PF-3 per-host memo blackholes NWS
(alerts included; one 5xx → 60–71 s cold start) · A11-1 NO_COLOR violated by kit-painted cells and not
named by the plan · BQ-1/RT-3 M1's statistic cannot detect the leak the problem statement is about ·

Critical / 53 Important across the formal lenses), and the plan of record was substantially rewritten. Recommendation: a
**single-agent** second round over v2 only (Code Quality axis + PLAN lens; personas only where v2
