# M3 — the ear test

**The metric.** Can a listener tell what KIND of alert is sounding, by ear, without
looking at the screen? It is the measurement the whole feature exists for: tones
per class are only worth having if the classes are distinguishable in the moment.

**Accepted by the HUM LEAD, 2026-09-06, on UAT evidence.**

> "I've heard different alert tones throughout my UAT sessions, so I hear the
> difference when a burst contains warnings vs. 'only contains watches' etc — not
> sure if that's sufficient, but from a UAT perspective I am satisfied with that
> functionality for this version."

## What this record is, and what it is not

**It is accumulated live listening, not a scripted trial.** `gates.md` §M3 asks for
*two trials, each recorded*, against 0.13.0. That is not what happened. What
happened is better in one way and weaker in another, and both belong here:

- **Better:** the evidence is many sessions of real alerts over days, on real
  weather, in the ordinary course of using the app — not two staged listens. The
  discrimination being reported is the one that matters, between bursts that
  differ in what they contain.
- **Weaker:** it is one listener, unblinded, who knows what the app is doing and
  wrote the rulings the tones implement. It establishes that the tones ARE
  distinguishable to someone attuned to them. It does not establish that a new
  listener learns them, and it is not a comparison against 0.13.0.

**So the claim this release may make is the narrow one:** the classes sound
different and the difference is audible in use. Any stronger claim — that a
listener can NAME the class by ear, or learns the set unprompted — is unmeasured.

## What would settle the stronger claim

A blinded trial: a listener who did not build the app hears bursts in a random
order and names the class, with the score recorded. Worth doing when the tone set
next changes, or before any marketing language asserts identification rather than
distinguishability. Recorded here rather than carried as a follow-up because the
gate is met for 0.14.0 — this is the note for whoever raises the bar.

**Verdict: M3 MET for 0.14.0.**
